# YACP 1.7.1

A small update with dictionary shortcuts and reading statistics display fixes.

### Added

- Dictionary lookup can now be assigned to a short or long Power press, a long
  Confirm press, or a long Back press while reading an EPUB. Existing button
  assignments are preserved.

### Fixed

- Reading statistics and book completion screens now show a single Home hint.
- The Total Reading Time label stays inside its card on the book completion screen.

### Changed

- The dictionary setup guide now explains installation after a firmware upgrade
  and how to resolve "No files found".

### Downloads

Choose the same font variant as your current installation. Both variants support X3 and X4.

- **Tiny**: 10, 12, 14, and 16 pt fonts.
- **XLarge**: 16, 18, and 20 pt fonts.

Open **Firmware Update** on your reader, or download the matching `.bin` below
and follow the [installation guide](https://github.com/Sichroteph/YACP/blob/v1.7.1-yacp/docs/installation.md).
Dictionary files are installed separately; see the [setup guide](https://github.com/Sichroteph/YACP/blob/v1.7.1-yacp/docs/dictionary.md).

### Hardware status

No new on-device X3 or X4 checks were performed for this update.
